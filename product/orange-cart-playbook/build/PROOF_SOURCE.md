## Key defensible insights (all real)
1. **Affiliate-first:** ~80% of GMV came from creators, not the brand account — in both shops.
2. **The ~$18/sample constant:** $18.38 vs $18.04 across two unrelated categories. This is the
   strongest proof the *system* (not the product) drives the result.
3. **Volume, not virality:** ~90% of videos produced zero sales; the winners carried the whole number.
4. **Screening is the lever:** approval rate ranged 7–30% — the single biggest cost control.
