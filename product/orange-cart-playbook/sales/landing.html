The shift

Stop trying to go viral.
Build the machine instead.

Across the portfolio, roughly 9 out of 10 creator videos sold nothing. It didn't matter — the tenth paid for all of them. Chasing one perfect video is buying a single lottery ticket.

The playbook is the opposite bet: put a hundred at-bats in a hundred feeds, screen who gets a sample, hand every creator a script built to convert, and scale only what the market already voted for.
